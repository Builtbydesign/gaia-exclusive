**Rubondo Island Villa** sits on Rubondo Island National Park in Lake Victoria — a forested island reserve reached only by boat or light aircraft. The villa is for exclusive use: one party, full privacy, and the island’s quiet shores, forest trails and lake light as your backdrop.

Days here move at island pace. Walk under the canopy, boat along the shoreline, fish, watch for chimpanzees and the island’s birdlife, then return to the villa for meals and long evenings by the water. There are no neighbouring lodges on your doorstep — only forest and lake.

## Why Rubondo Island

- True island privacy on a national park in Lake Victoria.
- Exclusive-use villa — the place is yours alone.
- Chimpanzee tracking and guided forest walks.
- Birding, boat outings and fishing on the lake.
- A complete change of scene from the northern safari circuit.
- Ideal as a quiet finale or interlude in a Tanzania itinerary.

## The Setting

Rubondo Island National Park · dense forest down to the shore · remote beaches and rocky points · classic Lake Victoria horizons
