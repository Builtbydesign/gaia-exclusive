Set in the Arusha highlands, **The Fort** is a small boutique lodge designed as a calm first or last stop on a northern Tanzania safari. Stone and brick architecture, quiet gardens, and views over the valley give it the feel of a private retreat rather than a transit hotel.

## Why The Fort

- A refined base at the start or end of your safari.
- Close to Arusha and the road to Lake Manyara, Tarangire and Ngorongoro.
- Characterful rooms with the atmosphere of a small fortress lodge.
- Gardens and outdoor spaces for rest between travel days.
- Thoughtful hospitality without the noise of a large hotel.

## The Stay

Expect an unhurried, private atmosphere: comfortable rooms, local character, and space to reset after a long flight or before heading into the parks. Meals and service are arranged to suit the pace of safari travel — early starts, late returns, and everything in between.

## Getting There

Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) is the usual arrival point · road transfers to The Fort on request · onward connections to the northern circuit by road or light aircraft
