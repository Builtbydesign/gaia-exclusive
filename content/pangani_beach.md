**Beach Under Canvas** is a small, private tented camp on the Pangani coast — where the river meets the Indian Ocean. Canvas, sand and sea air; a handful of tents set for exclusive or near-exclusive use, with the beach as the main stage.

This is the coastal chapter of a Tanzania journey: salt water after the dust of the bush, long horizons, and nights under canvas with the sound of the sea. Ideal at the end of a safari, or as a short, quiet escape on its own.

## Why Beach Under Canvas

- Tented camp on the Pangani shoreline — simple, atmospheric, close to the water.
- Small scale and a private, unhurried feel.
- Swimming, beach walks, dhow time and coastal village life nearby.
- A clear contrast to the northern parks and island bush.
- Easy to pair with a Serengeti or Arusha stay as a beach finale.

## The Setting

Pangani coast · river mouth and open ocean · sandy shore and coastal light · Swahili coast atmosphere

## Best For

Couples and small groups · post-safari beach time · travellers who prefer canvas and sand over a large resort

## The Stay

Comfortable tents, thoughtful camp service, and a rhythm set by the tide. Meals and boat or beach plans are arranged around your party. The emphasis is on the shore, the water and time to do very little.

## Getting There

Fly into Tanga or Kilimanjaro, then road transfer to Pangani · coastal transfers and timings arranged with your stay
