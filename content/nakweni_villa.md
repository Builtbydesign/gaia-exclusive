**Nakweni Porini** is a private villa on the northern boundary of the Serengeti, near Ikoma and Robanda. Built for exclusive use, it offers a quiet base in open country where wildlife moves freely through the surrounding wildlife management areas — and, in season, where the Great Migration passes.

The villa sits outside the national park boundary, so you keep the sense of the wild without the nightly park fees of a stay inside. Days can be as active or as still as you want: game drives, bush walks, time on the veranda, or riding out into the same country that surrounds the house.

## Why Nakweni Porini

- Completely private — the villa is yours alone.
- On the Serengeti edge, with wildlife in the management areas beyond the fence.
- Four of the Big Five in the area; all of the Small Five underfoot.
- Seasonal movement of the Great Migration through the region.
- Easy link to horseback safaris with the local riding outfit.
- A natural partner stay with Ikoma House for longer Serengeti itineraries.

## The Setting

Ikoma–Robanda on the northern Serengeti boundary · wildlife management areas on the doorstep · open country and classic savannah horizons

## Best For

Couples and small families · travellers who want privacy over a large camp · safari days mixed with time at the villa

## The Stay

Expect a private, staffed house rather than a hotel wing. Meals, guiding and daily rhythm are arranged around your party. The emphasis is on space, quiet, and direct access to the bush — with the option to add riding, walks and drives as you go.

## Horseback Safaris

Nakweni Porini is closely linked to the only horseback safari outfit on this stretch of the Serengeti. Rides leave into the same wildlife areas that surround the villa. Details and rates are on the [horse riding](#!horseriding) page.

## Getting There

Fort Ikoma airstrip is the nearest strip · road transfers on request · can be combined with Ikoma House for a two-base stay on the Serengeti boundary
