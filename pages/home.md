# Gaia Exclusive

A small collection of private villas and horse safaris on the edge of wild places

---

Gaia Exclusive is a handful of houses, not a hotel group. Each is private, fully staffed, and booked to one party at a time — a place to stay put, ride out from the door, and let the bush come to you.

We work on the boundaries: the wildlife management areas that fringe Serengeti National Park, the forested shore of Rubondo Island, the quiet stretch of coast at Pangani. Land that is protected, unfenced, and shared with everything that moves through it.

---

## THE HOUSES

**Ikoma House** — Serengeti National Park boundary
A private, fully staffed bush villa on 20 rewilded acres, with its own stable of six horses. Sleeps 8, full board.

**Nakweni Porini Villa** — Serengeti National Park boundary
The sister house to Ikoma, built for smaller parties and longer stays in the same wildlife management areas.

**Rubondo Island Villa** — Lake Victoria
Forest, water, and no one else. A villa on the shore of Rubondo Island National Park.

**Beach Under Canvas** — Pangani, Indian Ocean coast
Tented rooms on a quiet stretch of beach, with horses kept on the sand.

**The Fort** — Ikoma, Robanda
The original house, and the base for Nakweni Porini horse safaris.

---

## THE RIDING

We keep our own horses at each house, so you ride from the door rather than driving to a trailhead. Two hours, a half day, or a full day with a bush breakfast — guided at every level, and never more than a small group.

---

## THE WAY WE WORK

- One party at a time. No shared spaces with strangers.
- Full board, private chef, dedicated staff.
- No park or concession fees unless you choose a day inside the park.
- Replanted, rewilded land — 450+ indigenous trees at Ikoma House alone.
- Airstrip transfers arranged, from 15 minutes away.
